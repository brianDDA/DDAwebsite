NJ Licensed P&C Insurance Producer

Company: Don Dutton Agency LLC
Location: 780 Livingston Ave, North Brunswick, NJ 08902
Pay: $60,000.00–$70,000.00 per year
Job type: Full-time, in-office
Schedule: Monday through Friday, 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.

Don Dutton Agency is an independent insurance broker in North Brunswick, NJ. We specialize in commercial insurance and bonds for taxi and school transportation businesses and offer a range of personal and commercial insurance.

We are seeking a dependable, personable New Jersey licensed property and casualty producer who values long-term client relationships and personalized service.

Responsibilities

  • Quote and sell personal and commercial insurance policies.
  • Work with new and existing clients to identify coverage needs.
  • Prepare applications and proposals.
  • Communicate with insurance carriers and underwriters.
  • Follow up on leads and cross-selling opportunities.
  • Provide friendly, responsive customer service.

Requirements

  • Active New Jersey Property & Casualty Producer License.
  • Strong communication and customer service skills.
  • Organized and comfortable working with multiple carriers and systems.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience selling personal and/or commercial lines, including commercial auto, workers' compensation, or other small-business insurance.
  • Experience with both paper and digital filing systems.
  • Multilingual.

Benefits

  • Flexible schedule.
  • Paid time off.

How to Apply

Email your resume to jobs@donduttonagency.com.
