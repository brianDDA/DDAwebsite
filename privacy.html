Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 17, 2026

Don Dutton Agency, LLC ("Don Dutton Agency," "we," "us," or "our") respects the privacy of our clients, prospective clients, website visitors, and others who communicate with us. This Privacy Policy explains the types of personal information we may collect, how we use and disclose that information, and the measures we take to protect it.

Don Dutton Agency, LLC is a New Jersey insurance agency located at 780 Livingston Avenue, North Brunswick, NJ 08902. NJ Insurance License #1578726.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you provide to us when requesting an insurance quote, applying for insurance, purchasing or servicing an insurance policy, requesting a bond, communicating with our office, submitting documents, making a payment, using our website, or otherwise doing business with us.

Depending on the insurance product, service, or transaction, information we collect may include:

  • Name, mailing address, email address, and telephone number;
  • Date of birth and driver's license information;
  • Vehicle, driver, household, and property information;
  • Current and prior insurance information;
  • Policy and claims information;
  • Billing and payment-related information;
  • Business information, including ownership, operations, vehicles, employees, drivers, payroll, revenue, property, contracts, and loss history;
  • Information necessary to obtain insurance quotes, determine eligibility, submit applications, or service insurance policies;
  • Documents, photographs, forms, and other information you send to us; and
  • Other information that you voluntarily provide or that is reasonably necessary for an insurance transaction.

2. Sources of Information

We may obtain information directly from you and, when permitted by law, from other sources involved in an insurance transaction.

These sources may include:

  • Insurance companies;
  • Managing general agents and insurance wholesalers;
  • Insurance support organizations;
  • Consumer reporting agencies;
  • Motor vehicle and driver-record sources;
  • Government and public records;
  • Prior insurers;
  • Premium finance companies;
  • Payment providers;
  • Service providers; and
  • Other persons or organizations you authorize us to contact.

3. How We Use Information

We may use personal information to:

  • Respond to insurance inquiries;
  • Prepare and obtain insurance quotations;
  • Determine eligibility for insurance products;
  • Submit insurance applications to insurance companies, managing general agents, and wholesalers;
  • Bind or issue coverage when properly authorized;
  • Service, renew, modify, or cancel insurance policies;
  • Process certificates, endorsements, billing requests, payments, and other policy transactions;
  • Process bond requests;
  • Communicate with you about your insurance account;
  • Respond to document and service requests;
  • Detect or prevent fraud or unauthorized activity;
  • Maintain required business and insurance records;
  • Protect our clients, employees, systems, and business;
  • Comply with insurance, legal, and regulatory requirements; and
  • Operate and improve our business.

4. When We Disclose Information

We may disclose personal information when reasonably necessary to quote, place, administer, finance, pay for, or service insurance, or when otherwise permitted or required by law.

Recipients may include:

  • Insurance carriers;
  • Managing general agents and wholesalers;
  • Insurance support organizations;
  • Premium finance companies;
  • Payment processors;
  • Claims and underwriting service providers;
  • Consumer reporting and motor vehicle record providers;
  • Technology, communications, cybersecurity, and document service providers;
  • Professional advisers;
  • Governmental or regulatory authorities; and
  • Other organizations reasonably necessary to complete or service an insurance transaction.

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, regulatory request, or when reasonably necessary to prevent fraud, protect our rights or property, or protect the security of our clients and systems.

Don Dutton Agency does not sell personal information to data brokers or unrelated organizations for their independent marketing purposes.

5. SMS and MMS Privacy Policy

Don Dutton Agency may provide SMS and MMS messaging for customers and prospective customers who voluntarily choose to receive text messages from us.

SMS/MMS messages may relate to your insurance inquiry or account, including:

  • Quote and application follow-up;
  • Requests for documents or information;
  • Policy-service communications;
  • Billing and payment reminders;
  • Renewal reminders;
  • Policy cancellation or status notifications;
  • Certificates and endorsements;
  • Claims-related service communications;
  • Appointments or service reminders; and
  • Responses to questions or requests you send to us.
SMS Privacy: Mobile information, including mobile phone numbers, SMS/MMS opt-in information, and messaging consent, will not be shared, sold, rented, or disclosed to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

All information-sharing categories described elsewhere in this Privacy Policy expressly exclude SMS/MMS opt-in data and messaging consent for third-party or affiliate marketing or promotional use.

Providers that directly assist us in operating and delivering our messaging service, such as messaging platforms, telecommunications aggregators, and wireless carriers, may process information only as reasonably necessary to provide the messaging service.

We do not provide your SMS/MMS consent to unrelated organizations for their own use, marketing, promotional campaigns, or lead generation.

Verbal SMS/MMS Consent

We may obtain SMS/MMS consent verbally during a telephone conversation or in person at our office.

Before obtaining verbal consent, our staff will explain that:

  • Participation in SMS/MMS messaging is voluntary;
  • Message frequency may vary;
  • Message and data rates may apply;
  • You may reply STOP to unsubscribe;
  • You may reply HELP for assistance;
  • Your mobile information and messaging consent will not be shared for third-party or affiliate marketing or promotional purposes; and
  • Consent to receive SMS/MMS messages is not a condition of purchasing insurance or receiving services from us.

After you verbally opt in, we may send an SMS confirmation of your enrollment.

Opting Out of SMS/MMS

You may opt out of SMS/MMS communications at any time by replying STOP.

When you opt out, your customer record will be marked not to contact by SMS/MMS. We may send one final message confirming that your opt-out request has been processed.

After your opt-out is processed, we will not send additional SMS/MMS messages covered by that consent unless you subsequently provide new consent to receive them.

Opting out of SMS/MMS does not cancel an insurance policy or prevent you from communicating with Don Dutton Agency through other available methods, including telephone and email.

For assistance with messaging, reply HELP, call (732) 249-6346, or email csr@donduttonagency.com .

Additional messaging terms are available in our Messaging Terms & Conditions.

Contact our office if you need an appropriate method for transmitting sensitive information.
